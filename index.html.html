Selected work

Factory acceptance test: 34 faults caught before site

BMS/DDC panels for a pharma facility.

Problem
The panels had to ship with 7 days left in the delivery schedule. A hidden fault would have stalled commissioning at a regulated pharma site.
What I did
Energised each of the 6 panels and checked every terminal block. Verified AC and DC supply at input and output terminals and loop-tested the I/O. Logged 34 faults (voltage drops, loose connections, dead output terminals, indication LEDs not working) and issued them to the vendor in writing. My team and I stayed with the vendor’s team until every point was closed and re-tested.
Result
All 34 faults resolved before the panels left the factory.

Voltage measurement kit, loop tester, FAT checklists

CBS & TRS: cutting rework by 79%

Two quality controls I designed and introduced.

Problem
Documents and deliverables were going out with errors, causing rework loops and delays.
What I did
CBS (Check Before Share) is a mandatory self-check before anything leaves the team. TRS (Twice Review System) adds a second independent review before release. I piloted both, then rolled them out across projects.
Result
73% less rework in an 8-week pilot. 79% less across all projects in the 5-week rollout.

Lean Six Sigma, ISO 9001 audit principles, Excel tracking
